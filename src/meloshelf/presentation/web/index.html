Organize my music

Choose the folder with your music and where it should be organized. After you confirm, your files are moved: each one is verified at its destination before its original is removed.

Get ChromaprintChromaprint is a free tool that listens to a song to identify it. Without it MeloShelf still organizes music whose tags are reliable; everything else goes to review. Download the Windows zip from the official page, unzip it into C:\Tools\chromaprint, then choose Check again: no restart needed.

          Automatic artist enrichment

          No enrichment job yet. Names are looked up one at a time with the public MusicBrainz contact; each result is stored and reviewable.

          Artist photographs

          Photographs are fetched only for artists whose identity is verified, and from openly licensed images on Wikimedia Commons first; optional sources you switch on in Settings are only a fallback and always show their source. Each shows its author, licence and source. You can choose your own image, remove one, or undo your change. Nothing is written to your music files.

          Identity suggestions

          MusicBrainz artist IDs that MeloShelf cannot confirm by name alone. Accept one of the offered artists, reject it, or review it later. Your choice is stored apart from the provider's evidence, and undo reverses the last one.

          ACOUSTIC DISCOVERY

          Give every song a name.

          We analyze fingerprints locally and request candidate identities from AcoustID and MusicBrainz. You choose what is correct.

          ♫ ∿∿

          Find the music behind the filename

          Results are proposals, never automatically verified album editions. Files that already have a result are not analysed again.

            How it works

            1. 01
              Listen without uploadingChromaprint calculates a compact fingerprint on your device.
            2. 02
              Compare recordingsAcoustID returns possible matches, with raw match scores.
            3. 03
              Weigh the evidenceDuration and your existing tags are compared with each candidate. The MeloShelf score is a ranking aid, not a probability.
            4. 04
              You reviewAmbiguities and album editions are never silently resolved.

            PROTECT WHAT MATTERS

            Safety

            MeloShelf moves music only when you confirm an organization, and removes an original only after the file is verified at its destination. After a crash it recovers on its own.

            Exact copy

            Same bytes, same hash

            Not yet calculated. Exact duplicate detection comes in a later phase.

            Same recording

            Different encodings

            Coming later. A higher bitrate is not always better.

            Related version

            Live, remix, remaster

            Will be preserved as separate versions when the duplicate detector is implemented.

            Move engine · planned, not enabled

            Illustrative transaction phases. No file movement has been implemented.

            1. 1Prepared
            2. 2Staged
            3. 3Verified
            4. 4Published
            5. 5Source removed
            6. 6Committed

            This is the safety design target. Transaction logging, checksum verification and crash recovery must pass TDD before moves can be enabled.
